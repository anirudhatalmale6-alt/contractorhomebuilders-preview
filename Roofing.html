Choosing the right roof

California homeowners want a roof that keeps water out. Conventional shingle roofs, installed and repaired properly, can last two decades or more and are the least expensive option.

Metal and tile cost more but do a better job of repelling water, and they can last about 50 years without being replaced. When a roof can hold up for almost fifty years, spending more makes sense, and tile and metal roofs can even increase the resale value of your home.

Our roof selections

Find the right material.

Every material has its strengths. We help you choose the one that fits your home, style and budget.

Asphalt Shingles

Efficient to make, relatively simple to install and widely available, asphalt shingles are today's most popular roofing material. They cost less than wood, tile, metal or slate, and their warranted lifespan compares well with the alternatives.

Metal Roofing

We offer a wide variety of metal roofing products, used in residential, commercial, civic, agricultural, retail, healthcare and educational buildings.

Wood Shingles and Shakes

There are two kinds of wood roofing: shingles and shakes. Neither offers the practicality of modern materials like asphalt shingles, but it's hard to deny the classic appeal of natural wood.

Clay Tile and Concrete Tile

Concrete tile roofs aren't common, but they have many advantages over traditional materials like asphalt shingles. Concrete tile is very durable and resists damage from high wind, heat, hail and fire.

Architectural Shingles

Whether you want to match the timeless style of a traditional home or add a modern touch to a contemporary one, architectural roofing shingles make a roof replacement an exciting project.

Flat Roof

Like any roof, a flat roof has one job: keep your home dry and safe. It must protect your investment from rain, snow and the heat of the sun.
